02 — BUILD

Light doesn't mean fragile

Weight comes off where the shaft can afford to lose it. Strength stays where the game puts stress on it.

A

Tapered Wall

Thicker at the grip and throat transitions, thinner through the midsection — weight comes off exactly where it won't cost you stiffness.

B

Reinforced Ends

Butt-end and head connection points are built up, not shaved down — the two spots that take the hardest hits on the field.

C

Anodized Finish

A hard oxide surface layer for scratch and corrosion resistance — finished, not just painted.

We're not building one shaft for every position. This is a dedicated attack/midfield build — smaller diameter, quicker release, 30" length — because a shaft built for everyone ends up built for no one.
